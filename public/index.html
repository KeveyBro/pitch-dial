What's shared?

When a game ends, Pitch Dial sends its settings (answer mode, sound, range, listen and answer time) and, for each round, the target, your answer and the instrument. It sends no name, account, ID, location, exact time or audio, and results are only ever shown as overall comparisons. Sharing is on unless you turn it off here; it starts off if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal.
